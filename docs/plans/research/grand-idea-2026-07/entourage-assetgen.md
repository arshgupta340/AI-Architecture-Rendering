# entourage-assetgen — research + stress-test (2026-07-03)

**Headline:** Curate-over-generate is the right default for entourage in mid-2026: mature 2D cutout tools (many AI-assisted, several free) and Chaos Cosmos-style curated 3D libraries beat raw generative-3D for most entourage; generation wins for bespoke on-demand 2D figures, background props, and — as an emerging frontier — single-image-to-Gaussian-splat assets (TripoSplat) worth prototyping for hero shots.

## Key facts

- Chaos Cosmos expanded from ~6,000 to ~36,000 assets in July 2025 by acquiring/partnering with Globe Plants (plant library) and Evermotion, plus folding in Anima's 4,000+ animated people models (chaos.com press release, 2025-07-29)
- CSM (Common Sense Machines / Cube) was acquired by Alphabet/Google in January 2026; the team joined Google DeepMind (costbench.com, multiple 2026 sources)
- TripoSplat (VAST-AI-Research) converts a single 2D image directly to a 3D Gaussian splat, shipped native ComfyUI support June 1, 2026 (ComfyUI v0.23.0); free, self-hosted, outputs .splat/.ply viewable in Spark or SuperSplat
- Rodin Gen-2 (Hyper3D/Deemos) uses pay-per-download (not pay-per-generation) billing with unlimited free re-rolls, and includes commercial rights on its free tier -- most permissive terms surveyed
- Meshy AI: paid-tier users own generated assets outright and may resell them; free tier is CC BY 4.0 (commercial use allowed with attribution)
- World Labs Marble API prices a single Gaussian-splat world generation at 1,500-3,080 credits (~$1.20-$2.46) at $1/1,250 credits; returns .spz only
- Globe Plants closed its independent online store October 1, 2025 after being absorbed into Chaos Cosmos (cgchannel.com)
- Gendo (gendo.ai) already ships a free 236+-figure transparent-PNG cutout database plus paid Custom Cutout (posable AI figure generator) and Quick Populate (prompt-driven in-canvas people insertion with auto scale/lighting) -- a direct competitor already executing the 2D-entourage-generation strategy
- D5 Render 3.0 (2026) ships SmartPlanting (climate/site-aware auto planting + Auto Scatter), AI Asset Recommendation, and AI Model Generation that edits the live 3D scene rather than regenerating a flat image
- Standard chromakey workaround for transparent-background generation: prompt for solid #00FF00 background, then numpy/PIL alpha extraction with edge anti-aliasing -- no mainstream image model natively outputs alpha PNGs
- Tripo3D, Meshy, and Rodin all offer PBR-textured mesh output; reviewer consensus (dupple.com, 2026) ranks Rodin highest for final quality/least cleanup, Tripo best for post-edit topology, Meshy fastest/cheapest but weakest quality
- Industry-wide 2026 commentary (dancumberlandlabs.com, meltflexai.com, multiple studio blogs) documents a consistent 'too perfect/uncanny valley' failure mode for AI-generated content placed against precise real architectural geometry -- directly relevant risk for synthetic entourage in a geometry-locked scene

## Recommendation

Split by tier: (1) generate 2D people/cutouts on-demand via Nano Banana Pro/2 chromakey pipeline (~$0.03-0.10/figure) for foreground human entourage needing scene-specific demographics/pose/lighting -- cheap, proven by Gendo's existing product; (2) curate real 3D/scanned assets (Chaos Cosmos tier or Poly Haven/Quaternius CC0 tier) for any hero/foreground object an architect names specifically, since generated meshes still read as uncanny next to precise real geometry; (3) generate background/mid-ground fill (distant trees, generic street clutter) via mesh generators (Rodin Gen-2 one-offs or self-hosted Hunyuan3D 3.0 on Modal for volume) where quality bar is lower; (4) prototype TripoSplat now as a genuine differentiator -- free, self-hosted, Modal-and-Spark-compatible single-image-to-Gaussian-splat entourage that no current arch-viz competitor offers; (5) track Chaos closely as the incumbent already fusing curated-library + generative-AI + copilot in one product at $72-208/mo.

## Full report

## (a) 2D cutout generation
No image model natively outputs alpha-channel PNGs. Standard workaround (confirmed across multiple 2026 sources): prompt for a solid `#00FF00` background ("isolated on transparent background... just the object floating by itself, nothing else"), then chromakey + edge-antialias in post (dashbuilds.dev 2026-04-04; philschmid.de 2026-01; jidefr.medium.com 2025-12). Model tiers per dashbuilds.dev: `gemini-3-pro-image-preview` ("Nano Banana Pro") for quality-critical singles; `gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview` ("Nano Banana 2") for batch/4K/14-ref iteration; `gemini-2.5-flash-image` for cheap quick tests. Tripo's API prices premium image tiers (gpt-image-2, Midjourney, Gemini 3 Pro Image) at 10 credits (~$0.10); nano-banana at 3 credits (~$0.03).

Purpose-built arch tools already do this well — direct competitors/precedent: **Gendo** (gendo.ai) ships a free 236+-figure "Cutout Database" (transparent PNG people, filterable by pose/ethnicity/age/camera angle, no signup, commercial-use free) plus paid **Custom Cutout** (posable 3D mannequin → generated PNG) and **Quick Populate** (prompt-driven in-canvas people insertion with automatic scale/perspective/lighting match, <7s generation). **Rendair.ai** "Add Entourage" adds vehicles/street furniture via brush-select + prompt. **Adobe Firefly Generative Fill** is used ad hoc by firms directly in Photoshop for entourage/sky/context (archgyan.com 2026-04-07) — cited time savings: 2-3 days of post-production for 4 competition boards down to 1 day. Traditional paid cutout vendors (VIShopper, Toffu, Studio Alternativi, Post Digital Architecture, Arch Hive) still sell static PNG/vector packs ($15-50/pack) for vehicles and street furniture — these remain relevant for CAD-elevation-style flat cutouts that generative tools don't target well.

**Product implication:** 2D cutout generation is a solved, cheap problem (~$0.03-0.10/asset via Gemini image models) and Gendo has already built a competing product around exactly this. Differentiate on integration depth (auto scale/perspective/lighting match against the *real 3D scene*, which our pipeline already has via depth/camera buffers) rather than raw generation quality.

## (b) Image-to-3D / text-to-3D asset generation
Mesh-quality/cost landscape, verified pricing as of mid-2026:

- **Tripo AI** (tripo3d.ai): H3.1 is current flagship (4x mesh quality vs prior, PBR-ready, ~90s-5min). Smart Mesh (P1.0) generates clean low-poly quad topology in 2-5s for real-time use. Pricing: Free 200 credits/mo (~8 models); Pro $19.90/mo → 3,000 credits (~120 models); higher tiers to $109.90/mo. API: image-to-model 20-50 credits ($0.20-0.50), multiview-to-model similar. Commercial rights require paid tier; free tier is non-commercial/CC-BY-adjacent per some sources, contradicted by others — verify per-account before commercial use.
- **Meshy AI** (meshy.ai): Meshy 6 current gen, 20 credits/model+10 for texture = 20-30 credits total (~$0.40-0.60 at $20/1000-credit Pro rate). Free 100 credits/mo; Pro $20/mo (1,000 credits), Premium $40/mo (3,000), Ultra $100/mo (10,000). **Paid users own generated assets outright and can resell them**; free users get CC BY 4.0 (commercial use allowed with attribution) — notably permissive.
- **Rodin (Hyper3D/Deemos)**: Gen-2 (Aug 2025, BANG architecture, 10B params) is current best-in-class per multiple reviews — "4x mesh quality," native quad topology, recursive part-based generation, HD 4K textures, ~90s/generation. Nov 2025 update (Gen-2 1120) added seamless native-3D texturing (no dead-angle seams). Pricing: 10 free credits at signup, **pay-per-download not pay-per-generation** (unique UX advantage — unlimited free re-rolls before committing credits), Creator $20/mo. **Commercial rights included on free tier** — most permissive terms of any generator surveyed. Consensus across reviews (dupple.com, gaga.art, artificial-intelligence-wiki.com): Rodin = best final quality/least cleanup; Tripo = best editability/cleanest quads for Blender; Meshy = fastest/cheapest iteration but weakest quality, most cleanup needed.
- **Hunyuan3D 3.0** (Tencent, open-source, self-hostable): 2.6M+ downloads, most popular open-source 3D gen family; available via ComfyUI (Feb 2026). Zero marginal API cost if self-hosted on Modal — worth prototyping given existing Modal infrastructure.
- **CSM/Cube** (Common Sense Machines): **acquired by Alphabet/Google DeepMind, January 2026** — material consolidation signal, team joined Google DeepMind. Free tier 10 credits (CC BY 4.0); Maker $20/mo (100 credits, private ownership); Creative Pro $60/mo (400 credits, unlimited). Also has a "World Engine" for full 3D scene generation, relevant to (c)/(d) crossover.

**Quality-next-to-real-architecture caveat:** No source directly benchmarked AI-generated 3D entourage mesh quality against real architectural geometry, but the broader "AI renders look fake" literature (dancumberlandlabs.com, meltflexai.com, multiple studio blogs, all 2026) is unanimous that AI-generated content reads as "too perfect" / uncanny next to precisely-modeled real geometry — flat sourceless lighting, implausibly clean surfaces, inconsistent proportions across views. This is a systemic risk for *any* generated asset (2D or 3D) dropped into a geometrically precise Rhino/glTF scene: mismatched material fidelity and lack of photographic imperfection will read as fake faster than a scanned/curated asset would. This directly supports curating rather than generating for anything in the foreground/hero-shot frame.

## (c) Generated 3D Gaussian assets
This is the most novel and product-relevant finding. **TripoSplat** (VAST-AI-Research/TripoAI, github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSplat, paper arXiv:2605.16355) converts a single 2D image directly into a 3D Gaussian splat (up to 262,144 Gaussians), with **native ComfyUI support shipped June 1, 2026** (ComfyUI v0.23.0, official workflow template `3d_triposplat_image_to_gaussian_splat`). Runs locally/offline on any GPU (no API key), ~2,000 LOC, minimal dependencies (no `diffusers`/`transformers`). Outputs `.splat`/`.ply`, directly viewable in Spark (sparkjs.dev — **already your stack**) or SuperSplat. Important limitation: splats are not meshes — no rigging, no UV/PBR editing, no .obj/.fbx export; mesh-based tools (Tripo3D, InstantMesh) remain the right choice when a rigged/editable mesh is needed. Separately, arXiv:2508.21542 (Niantic/UCL, Aug 2025) demonstrates single-image scene-completion via latent diffusion for occluded-surface splat generation — relevant to your full-360 multi-view-consistency problem, since it's explicitly designed to hallucinate plausible unseen-side geometry rather than fail like your current reproject-from-3D approach does. **World Labs Marble** (worldlabs.ai, Fei-Fei Li) generates full explorable 3D worlds as Gaussian splats from text/image/video/pano; API returns `.spz` only. Pricing: $1/1,250 credits; a single world generation costs 1,500-3,080 credits depending on input type (~$1.20-$2.46/world) via API; subscription tiers Free (4 worlds)/Standard/Pro (25 worlds, export)/Max (75 worlds, custom pricing). This is your existing "gaussian-splat environment context" building block already in use — TripoSplat is the natural complement for individual object-level entourage assets rather than whole-scene backdrops.

**Product implication:** prototype TripoSplat locally (it's free, self-hosted, Modal-compatible) for generating individual splat entourage objects (a tree, a car, a bench) that composite into your existing Spark-based splat-backdrop pipeline — this is a genuinely under-explored niche relative to mesh-based generation, and matches your stack better than any mesh generator would.

## (d) Curated asset libraries — "curate, don't generate"
The market has consolidated hard around **Chaos Cosmos** (chaos.com/cosmos): free with any Chaos product subscription (V-Ray, Enscape, Vantage, Envision, Corona), grew from 6,000 to **~36,000 assets** after a July 2025 expansion that explicitly absorbed **Globe Plants** (the leading arch-viz plant library, which closed its independent store Oct 2025) and partnered with Evermotion, plus integrated **Anima's** 4,000+ animated 4D people models. Categories: vegetation, people, vehicles, furniture, accessories, HDRI skies, materials — essentially the entire entourage taxonomy in one curated, render-ready, cross-tool-compatible (V-Ray/Corona/Enscape/Vantage all share the same asset) library. Chaos also now bundles **AI credits** (Nano Banana 2 image gen, AI Material Generator from photos, "AI enhanced scenes") directly into the same subscription tiers ($72-208/mo), i.e. Chaos itself is fusing curated + generative in one product — this is the most direct precedent for your own strategy and worth studying as a competitor.

Free CC0 libraries remain excellent for background/mid-ground fill at zero cost: **Poly Haven** (fully CC0, no signup, HDRIs+models+textures), **ambientCG** (CC0 PBR materials/HDRIs/models), **Quaternius** (CC0 low-poly asset packs, strong for stylized/whitebox work), aggregated via **3DAssetGrid**. These lack photorealistic scanned-quality hero assets but are zero-friction for filler entourage (background trees, generic street objects).

Paid specialist libraries: **3DPeople** (photoscanned humans, 50k-mesh, PBR, ready-posed and ready-animated, ~3.1 billion source pixels/figure — explicitly positions against AI generation on realism grounds), **Chaos Anima** (4,000+ animated 4D people + traffic simulation, now folded into Cosmos), **MetaHuman** (Unreal, free, rigged photoreal humans — heavier pipeline, real-time-engine-specific), **VIShopper** (cutout people/trees/textures, the original arch-viz cutout vendor).

## (e) Scatter/placement UX precedents
**Lumion 2026.0** shipped an **Area Placement** tool (up to 5,000 nature items, 20 asset types, per operation) with density/collision/weight controls and "conform to ground" — direct precedent for non-destructive scatter-paint UX. Library: 7,500+ assets, 1,468+ PBR materials, 96 upgraded photogrammetry tree models in 2026.0 alone. **Enscape**: 3,500+ assets, 425 PBR materials (via Chaos Cosmos integration since Sept 2025, now 10,000-18,500+ via Premium/Collection tiers). **Twinmotion**: leverages Quixel/Fab Megascans integration for photogrammetry-scanned objects/materials (Fab pricing since Epic's 2024-2025 Quixel→Fab migration: standalone assets from $0.99, packs from $24.99, Standard License Personal free under $100k revenue). **D5 Render 3.0** (2026) is the most AI-forward: **SmartPlanting** (site/climate-aware auto-generated planting plans + Auto Scatter refinement), **AI Asset Recommendation** (image/text → matching library asset), and **AI Model Generation** (text/image → custom 3D asset placed directly into the live scene, not a flat image) — explicitly positioned as "scene-native AI control" that edits real scene parameters (environment/materials/lights) rather than regenerating a flat image, which is philosophically identical to your own non-destructive-layer thesis.

## Recommendation
1. **2D entourage (people, background props): generate on-demand, don't curate.** Nano Banana Pro/2 chromakey-cutout pipeline costs ~$0.03-0.10/figure, matches your existing image-gen tooling, and lets you match demographics/pose/lighting to the specific scene — Gendo has already proven this exact workflow's product-market fit. Build this as a thin feature, not a moat.
2. **Foreground/hero 3D objects (a specific bench, a specific tree species the architect names): curate, don't generate.** License Chaos Cosmos-tier or Poly Haven/Quaternius-tier assets. Current AI 3D mesh generators (even Rodin Gen-2) don't clear the bar for photorealistic assets sitting directly beside precise, real, geometrically-locked architecture — the "too perfect / uncanny" failure mode documented industry-wide applies doubly to synthetic entourage next to real geometry.
3. **Background/mid-ground fill vegetation and generic street furniture: generate via mesh (Rodin Gen-2 for one-offs, or self-hosted Hunyuan3D 3.0 on Modal for volume/zero marginal cost) or scatter from CC0 libraries** — quality bar is lower at scatter density and distance, so generation cost/speed wins.
4. **Prototype TripoSplat now** (free, self-hosted, Modal-compatible, Spark-compatible) as the differentiated bet: single-image-to-splat entourage objects that composite natively into your existing Gaussian-splat backdrop pipeline is not something any current arch-viz competitor (Chaos, D5, Lumion, Gendo) is doing yet.
5. **Watch Chaos** closely — it's the incumbent doing exactly your "curate + generate + copilot" fusion strategy already, at $72-208/mo, backed by ~36,000 curated assets plus bundled Nano Banana 2 credits and an AI material-from-photo tool.

## Adversarial stress-test (Opus 4.8)

**Verdict:** viable-with-changes

**Fatal flaws:**

- none

**Risks:**

- THE HEADLINE 'DIFFERENTIATOR' IS THE WEAKEST PART. The researcher's most-hyped bet — TripoSplat single-image-to-splat entourage as 'a genuine differentiator no arch-viz competitor offers' — collapses on the revision problem the prompt explicitly names. Verified: a Gaussian splat bakes lighting at generation time. Per CG Lounge (2026-04) 'you cannot add a key light, remove a shadow, change time of day, or match to a plate with different lighting — this is architectural, not a limitation you can prompt away,' and aukimi (2026-04) 'your splat scene looks great at the time of day you captured, and ONLY at that time of day.' A splat tree generated for a 4pm golden-hour hero shot is unusable the moment the architect switches the suncalc sun to overcast morning — which this product does routinely. It also can't be relit to match the FLUX hero render's lighting, so it will read as a foreign object pasted into the scene. The 'differentiator no competitor offers' is unoffered partly because it is a bad fit for a lighting-variable architectural tool. It's a demo trick, not a product primitive.
- COMPOUNDING UNCANNY-VALLEY ERROR IS SYSTEMIC, NOT PER-ASSET. The researcher flags 'too perfect' as one risk among many. Verified across 6 independent 2026 sources (ArchDaily/Maganga, Dan Cumberland Labs, MeltFlex, DesignScene, ArchitectGPT, Motif) that this is the UNANIMOUS, defining failure mode: weightless figures not touching ground, sourceless flat lighting, plastic materials, no cultural/local specificity. Critically for THIS product: each generated asset carries its own independent lighting/material error, and they DON'T share a light transport model with the geometry-locked FLUX hero render or the WebGPU real-time scene. So errors don't average out — they stack. A scene with a generated 2D cutout person + a generated splat tree + a Rodin mesh bench has three mutually-inconsistent lighting signatures against one precise real building. Sophisticated clients (the sources stress 'the brain registers the mismatch before the conscious mind can name it') read this as fake faster than any single asset would in isolation.
- GENDO IS NOT 'PROOF OF PMF' — IT IS A HEAD-START COMPETITOR ALREADY SHIPPING THE ENTIRE THESIS. Verified: Gendo ships a free 236+ cutout database, Custom Cutout (posable 3D-mannequin figure gen), Quick Populate (<7s in-canvas people with auto scale/perspective/lighting via 'vision-aware segmentation'), an infinite in-browser canvas, region Smart-Selection material edits, AND a natural-language 'Gendo AI Assistant' copilot with tool access. That is items (a), (d), and (e) of the grand idea, plus the copilot from (d)-of-the-vision, already in market. The researcher frames Gendo as validation; it is more accurately an incumbent who has executed the 2D-entourage + copilot strategy the founder is still planning. The founder's claimed edge ('auto-match against the REAL 3D depth/camera buffers') is real but narrow, and Gendo is already doing perspective/lighting match on 2D renders without needing the buffers.
- THE MOAT IS A THIN FEATURE THE RESEARCHER ADMITS. By the researcher's own recommendation, 2D cutout gen is 'a solved, cheap problem' to 'build as a thin feature, not a moat,' foreground 3D should be CURATED not generated, and background fill is commodity mesh gen or CC0 scatter. Strip those out and the entire entourage-assetgen dimension contributes ZERO defensibility to the grand idea. The only novel bet (splat entourage) is the one that fails the lighting/revision test above. So this dimension is table-stakes-fill, not a differentiator — the grand idea cannot lean on it for a moat, only for feature parity.
- INCUMBENT RESPONSE IS ALREADY HERE AND IS SCENE-NATIVE. Verified: D5 Render 3.0 (Jan 2026) ships SmartPlanting (climate/site-aware auto-planting), AI Asset Recommendation, AI Image-to-3D that edits the LIVE scene, AND direct .ply/.gs Gaussian-splat import (alpha) — meaning D5 already ingests exactly the splat assets the founder wants to differentiate on. Chaos Cosmos (~36k assets) bundles Nano Banana 2 credits + AI-material-from-photo into one $72-208/mo subscription across V-Ray/Enscape/Corona/Vantage. The incumbents' scene-native AI (edit real scene params, not a flat image) is philosophically identical to the founder's own non-destructive-layer thesis — they arrived first, with 36k curated assets and cross-tool reach the solo dev cannot match. The grand idea's parallel-track (e) 'integrate into incumbents' is sound, but on entourage the incumbents don't need the founder.
- LICENSING IS A LATENT LIABILITY, NOT A SOLVED PROBLEM. Verified: no mainstream image model outputs native alpha (chromakey #00FF00 workaround required — an antialiasing/edge-fringe quality tax at print resolution). Nano Banana consumer wrappers push ALL indemnity onto the user ('you agree to indemnify... arising out of your Generated Images'). Generator commercial terms are inconsistent and self-contradicting across sources (Tripo free-tier commercial status is 'contradicted by others — verify per-account'). For a tool whose output goes into planning submissions, competition boards, and client deliverables where a licensed architect's standard of care is on the line (AIA Trust guidance, insurer-named 'physically impossible output' risk per Dan Cumberland Labs), shipping mixed-provenance generated assets with per-provider license drift is a real exposure the researcher underweights.
- COST/LATENCY IS FINE PER-ASSET BUT UNMODELED AT SCENE SCALE. $0.03-0.10/figure and ~90s-5min/mesh are demo numbers. A single hero board needs dozens of figures + trees + street furniture + revisions. At 5-10 revisions per project (verified: exterior elevations 'shift three times in a morning' during DD, per Chaos), every 'move the window 2ft' can invalidate baked splat entourage and force regeneration of lighting-matched cutouts. The researcher never multiplies unit cost by asset count x revision count x view count — the real figure for a delivered project is 1-2 orders of magnitude above the quoted per-asset price, and latency compounds against real deadlines.

**Recommendations:**

- DEMOTE TripoSplat splat-entourage from 'the differentiated bet' to a fenced R&D experiment explicitly gated on relightable-splat research shipping to mainstream tools (GHPT/relightable-GS at CVPR 2026 exist but are research-stage). Do NOT build product roadmap on it. It fails the lighting-variable + revision workflow this product lives in. If prototyped, restrict to fixed-lighting single-hero-shot marketing stills only, never the interactive scene.
- Reframe this entire dimension internally as TABLE STAKES, not a moat. Adopt the researcher's tier split (generate 2D, curate hero 3D, commodity-fill background) as the CHEAPEST path to feature parity with Gendo/D5/Chaos — and put the actual moat elsewhere (the geometry-locked FLUX hero + per-region byte-stable re-roll + Rhino-native depth/id buffers, which no competitor replicates). Entourage should be a checkbox, funded minimally.
- Make lighting-coherence the product's entourage differentiator instead of generation novelty. The one genuine edge is that you own the depth/normal/id/camera buffers and the scene's actual sun (suncalc). Build entourage insertion that RE-LIGHTS or re-renders the asset through the same FLUX/ControlNet pass that produced the hero, so the person/tree shares the scene's exact light transport. That directly attacks the unanimous 'weightless, sourceless, plastic' failure mode — and it's something Gendo (2D-only, no true 3D lighting) structurally cannot do.
- Treat the revision case ('move the window 2ft after renders are done') as the primary design constraint, not an edge case. Entourage assignments must be non-destructive layers keyed to scene regions/anchors that survive geometry edits and re-render deterministically — mirroring the existing region->material layer model. Any entourage approach (splat especially) that has to be re-generated from scratch on a geometry change is disqualified for the interactive scene.
- Resolve licensing BEFORE any client-deliverable path ships: standardize on the most permissive verified terms (Meshy paid = outright ownership+resale; Rodin free-tier commercial + pay-per-download re-rolls; self-hosted Hunyuan3D 3.0 on Modal = no license drift at all) and route through those deliberately. Prefer self-hosted-on-Modal generation (zero marginal cost AND clean provenance) over consumer Nano Banana wrappers that dump indemnity on the user.
- Study Gendo as the competitor to beat, not the validator. It has already shipped 2D entourage + copilot + canvas + region edits. Assume feature parity is the floor; win on the two things it lacks — true 3D scene grounding (real buffers, real sun) and Rhino-native host integration — not on entourage generation quality, which is commoditized.
- Model real per-project cost: unit price x (figures+props+veg) x views x revision rounds. Cap generation spend by defaulting to CC0 curated scatter (Poly Haven/Quaternius/ambientCG) for background/mid-ground and reserving paid generation for named foreground assets only, exactly as the researcher recommends — but enforce it as a hard budget rule given the $0.05/session discipline already in the repo.
