# E3 — Swatch-conditioning shootout (house frame, tier-1 data)

- **Task:** "Does travertine read as travertine" — four swatch-conditioning methods on the E2 depth-locked render, masked by the host's ground-truth wall mask, swatch = `spike/test_assets/travertine.jpeg`.
- **Status:** **GATE PASSED — `flux2_multiref` + mask composite.** Wall reads unambiguously as coursed beige stone (travertine); everything outside the mask stays pixel-identical via `composite.paste_tile`.
- **Cost:** ~$0.30 (fill $0.05, IP-Adapter $0.08 + 2 failed validations unbilled, FLUX.2 Edit $0.06, Kontext multi $0.08). Ledger total ≈ $2.22 of $50.
- **Date:** 2026-06-12

## Results (evidence: `e3_wall_zoom.png`, `e3_audit_stack.png`, `final_composite.png`)

| candidate | material identity | locality/alignment | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **flux2_multiref** (FLUX.2 [pro] Edit, [render, swatch]) | **travertine: coursing, pitting, cream tone** | held camera; diff 59.5 inside mask vs 16.2 outside → residue killed by mask composite | **WINNER** |

## The production recipe (v1 apply-material stage)

```
edit  = FLUX.2 [pro] Edit(image_urls=[base_render(JPEG), swatch], prompt="apply material … keep everything else identical")
final = composite.paste_tile(base_render, host_wall_mask, edit)
```
- The mask composite makes whole-image-editor residue irrelevant — only masked pixels land.
- Cost per material swap: **$0.06**, ~80s. MatSwap-on-Modal contingency NOT needed for v1.
- fal gotchas captured in `run_e3_swatch.py`: data-URI payloads must be JPEG-compressed (413 otherwise); XLabs IP-Adapter needs `weight_name` + `image_encoder_path`; Kontext multi can't be trusted with alignment.
