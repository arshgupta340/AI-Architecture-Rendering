## Findings

1. **Hosted-stack gotchas (reusable knowledge):** community Replicate models 404 on the model-path endpoint — use version-pinned `/v1/predictions` (now handled in `probe_grounded_sam.py`); `meta/sam-2` on Replicate is the *automatic* mask generator and ignores box prompts (returns whole-image masks); `schananas/grounded_sam` returns `[annotated, neg_annotated, mask, inverted_mask]` — take `mask.jpg`, not last; it crashes on zero detections; Replicate 429s need backoff.
2. **Mullion direction is right, precision isn't.** grounded_sam found the glazing zones (47% recall) but masks are coarse blobs (84k px vs 3.7k GT) — usable maybe for click-disambiguation, useless for material edits.
3. **Domain mismatch dominates.** Detectors are photo-trained; the input was a flat-shaded CAD viewport. T21 measured the same effect for Gemini (0 windows on raw screenshots, 77 on photoreal). **Action: re-run this probe on the E2 photoreal render of the same frame before concluding on tier-2.** Also pending: true SAM 3 (not on Replicate yet) and Florence-2 grounding, which research ranks above Grounding-DINO for this.

## Standing conclusion for the master plan

On identical pixels: plugin ground truth (93.1% exact, per-instance) vs best generative probe (mullion IoU 0.003) vs hosted discriminative stack (mullion IoU 0.02). **Nothing in tier 2 approaches tier 1** — the pivot's premise gets stronger with each probe. Tier 2 remains necessary only as the no-plugin fallback, and its model choice should be re-evaluated on photoreal inputs (post-E2).
