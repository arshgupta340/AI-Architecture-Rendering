## Quality findings — Spike 3 gate does NOT pass on this single render

A visual review of `tagged_render.png` surfaced several substantive issues. Counting *bboxes returned* is not the right metric; counting *bboxes that are correctly labeled and tightly placed* is. By that metric:

### Issue A — Bboxes are in normalized 0–1000 coordinates, not pixels

Render dimensions: **1259×848**. But Gemini's bboxes max out at x≈1000, y≈1000:

- r31 "ground": `x=0, y=709, w=1000, h=291` → implicit right edge at x=1000, bottom at y=1000.
- r9 "wall": `x=778, w=222` → right edge at x=1000.

Gemini 3 Pro is returning **normalized 0–1000 coordinates** (standard VLM behavior for spatial outputs). The visualization in `test_vlm_tagging.py:_draw_regions` treats these as raw pixel coordinates of the 1259×848 render. That's a coordinate-space bug: every bbox is drawn at ~79% of its correct width and ~118% of its correct height.

**Fix path:** either (a) instruct Gemini to use actual pixel coordinates of the input image (and verify it complies — VLMs often ignore this and normalize anyway), or (b) scale bboxes from 1000-space to pixel-space in the visualization and downstream SAM2 prompts. Option (b) is safer.

### Issue B — Confidence is flat 0.95 across all 49 regions

Every single region returned `confidence=0.95`. That's a flat line, which means the field is not informative — the model isn't actually self-rating. We can't use confidence to filter.

### Issue C — "Wall" bboxes are too large to be useful

`r1 "wall" at (655, 0, 345, 571)` covers nearly the entire right portion of the image *including* the neighboring building's windows and balconies. That's not one wall; that's a building plus its windows. As a SAM2 prompt this is useless — SAM2 would segment whatever is most salient inside the box, which is unpredictable.

### Issue D — Many "door" labels are actually balcony glazing or windows

- r34–r38 (labeled "door", parented to windows r13–r17 on the right facade): these are window/balcony glazing units on the *neighboring* building, not architectural doors. Even granting that interpretation as French-door balconies, they're on the wrong building.
- r39, r40, r41 (labeled "door" on the left side): placed on what appear to be balcony elements on upper floors of the main building, not doors.

### Issue E — Hierarchy is sometimes wrong

`r33 "door" parent=r24 "window"` — placing a door inside a window is geometrically incoherent. The model is using parent-child to encode "spatial overlap," not "containment relationship," which is not what the schema means.

## Revised Spike 3 gate assessment

**Plan's gate criterion:** "Gemini 3 Pro correctly tags ≥ 80% of major architectural regions (walls, windows, mullions, floors, ceilings, doors) on 5 diverse test screenshots."

We have:
- **1 test screenshot** (not 5)
- **Coordinate-space bug** that invalidates spatial accuracy of every bbox
- **Confidence field unusable** (flat 0.95)
- **Wall bboxes too loose** to drive SAM2
- **Mullion label not returned at all** (may be acceptably subsumed into windows; needs a Spike-3-with-mullions test to confirm)
- **Several door mislabels**

This is a **smoke-test pass** (the pipeline runs end-to-end, schema validates, the model returns plausible *labels* for most major surface categories) but **not a Spike 3 gate pass**. The bbox quality is not yet good enough to drive Spike 4's "click a tagged region → swap material" flow without a coordinate fix and a prompt revision.
